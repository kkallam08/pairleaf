## Name

Pairleaf - PDF study reader

## Short description

Read PDF questions with their original solutions beside them. Open a PDF pair or textbook and save study sets on your device.

## Detailed description

Keep the question in view. Open its original solution beside it.

Pairleaf is a PDF study reader for question papers and textbooks. Open separate question and solution PDFs, or one textbook with an answer section. Reveal an answer when you are ready, then return to the same reading position.

• Match numbered questions using question numbers and chapter or exercise context.
• Reveal original solution regions, including answers that continue across pages.
• Detect a textbook's answer section and correct its page range with previews.
• Review unresolved matches and choose the correct source solution.
• Keep PDFs in their folders and save match choices, reading position, and zoom in a local study library.
• Export complete study sets as .pairleaf backups and restore them on another installation.

PDFs are read from the files you select; study progress is saved in Chrome. Pairleaf does not upload documents or collect analytics. Reading works offline, with no account, API key, or separate helper.

The current version supports upright, selectable-text PDFs with a single column and consistent numbered questions. For a textbook, answers must occupy one contiguous page range after the question pages. Scans, handwriting, password-protected documents, and complex column layouts are not supported. Matching can be wrong or incomplete; review the source when needed. Pairleaf reveals existing solutions and does not generate answers or check their correctness.

The reader displays original PDF pages as images. Full PDF text selection, active links, printing, and screen-reader access to the document text are not implemented. The reader controls support keyboard navigation.

Study progress belongs to the current Chrome profile and extension installation. Keep the linked PDFs or imported backup file available. Chrome may require renewed file access; Locate files lets you reconnect verified originals. Changed PDFs need a new study. Later progress does not update an imported backup until you export again. Export a backup before uninstalling or clearing extension data. Backups include the original PDFs and are not encrypted.
